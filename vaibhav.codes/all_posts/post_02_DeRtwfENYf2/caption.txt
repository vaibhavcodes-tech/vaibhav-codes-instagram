The BIGGEST AI Problem No One Is Talking About? 👇

It’s called CONTEXT ROT and it’s why your AI gets DUMBER the longer you talk to it.

We think giving AI more info = smarter answers. Wrong.

Research by Chroma (tested 18 frontier models) proved it:
A 300-token focused prompt BEAT a 113k-token long prompt. Every single time.

Why?
Your context window is NOT a storage bin. It’s an attention budget.

Every extra token you add, the AI pays less attention to everything else. It starts forgetting instructions, mixing data, and hallucinating — silently.

This is why your long agents, RAG systems, and 10-page prompts fail.

Fix? Context Engineering > Prompt Engineering.

90% of devs fail this Flipkart-style interview question. 👇

How do you stop one user from hitting login 1000 times?

Most say “rate limiting” and stop there. The interviewer wants more:

❌ Fixed Window: 100 requests at 11:59:59 + 100 at 12:00:01 = 200 in 2 seconds
✅ Token Bucket: allows normal bursts, enforces the steady limit
⚠️ 10 servers with their own buckets = 1000 requests (fail)
🔥 Fix: one shared bucket in Redis, updated with a Lua script
📩 Always send headers: how many requests are left, and when to retry
